# Guidon social profile assets

- `profile-avatar.png` — 400 × 400 transparent PNG with the Guidon Blue symbol and extra clearance for circular profile crops.
- `profile-avatar.svg` — vector source for the profile image.
- `x-header.png` — 1500 × 500 X profile header.
- `linkedin-cover.png` — 1584 × 396 LinkedIn cover.
- `youtube-channel.png` — 2560 × 1440 YouTube channel banner. The wordmark and tagline sit inside the center safe area.

The banners share a dark context-network background and the full white wordmark. Use the full wordmark on banners and the symbol-only avatar for profile photos. The slogan is “Keep the why with the work.”
