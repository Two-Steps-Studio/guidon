# Code of Conduct

We want Guidon to be a friendly place for everyone, whatever their experience,
background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Discuss ideas, not people. Disagree politely and explain why.
- Assume good intent and accept feedback gracefully.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language.
- Sexual content or unwanted attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions or ignoring a maintainer's request to stop.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other Guidon
community space. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and block people who
break these rules.

Report problems to **support@useguidon.com**. Reports are handled privately.
