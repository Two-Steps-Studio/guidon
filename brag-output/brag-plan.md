# Brag Plan: Guidon

## What is this app?
Guidon is context-first project management for development teams: tasks live alongside decisions, knowledge, and project context.

## The angle
Show the work, then reveal the reasoning and memory attached to it. Guidon's own line, “Keep the why with the work,” supplies both the hook and the promise. Real screens make the promise concrete; the closing beat names REST and MCP access for AI agents without inventing capabilities.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The Guidon wordmark resolves into “Keep the why with the work.” The statement is the value proposition and opens directly on the product's own positioning.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The dashboard crop shows “Your open tasks” and “Up next for you,” without sample profile names or the activity feed.
- The decisions screen shows a real example with its rationale, impact, alternatives, and approval state.
- The memory screen shows persistent project knowledge and a reviewable AI insight; the caption calls out REST + MCP for agents.

## Outro / punchline
Return to the Guidon wordmark and the exact tagline, with `useguidon.com` as the action.

## User flow worth showing
Open the task board → inspect a recorded decision and why it was made → keep project knowledge available and connect an authorized agent through REST or MCP.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: quiet, confident developer-tool launch film
- Interpretation: clean feature proof with measured motion, specific product copy, and a restrained sonic bed.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20.4 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#0b0d10`
- Accent: `#4d8dff` with brand blue `#1d4fd8`
- Text: `#e8eaed`
- Display font: Montserrat (video-safe companion to the app's Geist sans)
- Body font: Montserrat; technical labels: JetBrains Mono
- Strongest visual element: the task dashboard, followed by the decision rationale and project-memory cards.

## Share copy (draft)
Tasks say what needs to happen. Guidon keeps the decisions and context that explain why.

## Audio direction
- Role: restrained, polished music bed with sparse soft accents.
- Music: “Happy Beats / Business Moves,” vol. 12 by ende.app.
- Music treatment: begin at 0s, fade up over 0.6s to 0.32, hold beneath the UI, fade to silence over the final 0.8s.
- Music cue guidance: bundled cue file is in `composition/assets/music/cues/`. Major scene changes land at 8.74s, 13.11s, and 17.47s; these are strong cues in the first 20.4 seconds. The first product-screen reveal lands on the 3.27s beat-grid point.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle change in the brand-blue light around the Guidon end card follows precomputed bass energy.
- SFX posture: sparse and quiet; three soft interface/reveal accents and one restrained end-card accent.
- Audio-coupled moments: screenshot entrances and final wordmark land on selected beats.
- Restraint rule: no voiceover, no bright repeated clicks, and no sound louder than the product message.
