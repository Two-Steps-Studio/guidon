# Data Processing Addendum — negotiation draft

Version 2026-10-25. **Not executed. Not incorporated into Cloud terms by publication.** Complete the parties, annexes and authorisation mechanism and obtain legal review before offering this agreement for acceptance. No vendor contract, transfer instrument or technical safeguard is certified by this draft.

## 1. Parties and scope

Customer/controller: legal name, address, authorised representative and privacy contact **to be completed**. Guidon provider/processor: legal name, address, authorised representative and privacy contact **to be completed**. Underlying service order, effective date and acceptance/signature evidence **to be completed**.

This addendum applies to personal data processed solely on the controller's behalf through the agreed Guidon Cloud workspace. It does not reclassify the provider's independent account, billing or statutory processing as processing on the customer's instructions. GDPR terms retain their statutory meaning. Mandatory data-protection obligations prevail over conflicting service terms.

## 2. Documented instructions and confidentiality

The processor processes data only on documented lawful instructions, including authorised service configuration and written instructions for transfers, except where Union or Member State law requires otherwise. It informs the controller of that requirement before processing unless legally prohibited. It immediately informs the controller if an instruction appears to infringe applicable data-protection law and seeks clarification before proceeding with the affected instruction.

Persons authorised to process data must be subject to confidentiality duties and receive access only as needed for their role. This agreement does not authorise sale of project content or training a general-purpose AI model on it.

## 3. Security and incidents

The processor implements appropriate Article 32 measures, considering risks, state of the art, cost and processing context, as specifically agreed and evidenced in Annex B. Measures must be kept appropriate as risks change. No unverified certification or absolute security guarantee is implied.

The processor notifies the controller of a personal-data breach without undue delay after becoming aware of it, provides available information needed for the controller's legal duties, supplements incomplete information without undue delay and cooperates in containment and remediation. A processor notice does not replace the controller's assessment or statutory notification deadlines.

## 4. Assistance

Taking account of the nature of processing, the processor assists through appropriate technical and organisational measures with data-subject requests. It forwards requests relating to controller data without undue delay and does not decide them independently except on instructions or legal obligation. Taking account of information available, it assists with security, breach notifications, impact assessments and prior consultation under Articles 32–36.

## 5. Subprocessors

Select and document specific prior written authorisation or general written authorisation before execution. For general authorisation, Annex C must specify the initial approved list, advance change notice period, notification address and a meaningful opportunity to object before the new processing starts. Unresolved objections require an agreed lawful resolution; these terms do not erase the controller's statutory rights.

The processor imposes materially equivalent applicable data-protection duties by written contract on each subprocessor and remains fully liable to the controller for the subprocessor's performance of those duties. A customer-selected integration is assessed according to its actual role rather than automatically listed as a subprocessor.

## 6. International transfers

No restricted transfer takes place without documented instructions and an applicable Chapter V mechanism and necessary supplementary measures. Annex C must identify actual locations and safeguards. This draft does not itself execute Standard Contractual Clauses or claim that a provider's certification covers this deployment. Complete any necessary transfer instrument, parties, modules, annexes and assessment separately.

## 7. Return, deletion and duration

Processing lasts for the service period and any specifically agreed lawful wind-down in Annex A. At the controller's choice the processor returns or deletes personal data after the end of processing and deletes existing copies unless Union or Member State law requires retention. The parties specify a practical export window, active-system deletion schedule, backup expiry and legal-retention restrictions in Annex B before execution. Retained data must be protected and restricted to the required purpose. Deleting an individual user does not itself instruct deletion of an entire shared organisation.

## 8. Demonstrating compliance and audits

The processor makes available all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with Article 28 and allows and contributes to audits, including inspections, by the controller or its mandated auditor. Proportionate scheduling, confidentiality and protection of other customers may be agreed but must not defeat these rights, urgent investigations or supervisory authority access. It promptly flags unlawful instructions as described above.

| Purpose | Customer's project/work management; confirm instructed purposes |

## Annex B — measures and lifecycle (evidence required)

Record actual access/tenant isolation, privileged access controls, transport/storage protection, secret management, logging/redaction, vulnerability handling, incident process, staff confidentiality, backup/restore, data-subject assistance, export and deletion procedures. For each, identify implemented controls and responsible owner; do not mark planned controls as implemented. Fill retention/backup periods, deletion verification and exceptions, restore safeguards and processing locations.

## Annex C — authorised processors and transfers (verify each row)

Inventory candidates include hosting/delivery (Vercel), database/auth/storage (Supabase), transactional email (Resend), diagnostics (Sentry if enabled) and any instructed AI or other processing. Determine each recipient's actual role; payment provider independent-controller processing may belong outside this DPA. For every actual subprocessor list legal entity, function/data, processing countries, contract, authorisation and transfer mechanism with evidence. **No candidate is authorised by this uncompleted annex.**
